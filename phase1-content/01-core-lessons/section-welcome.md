## Section title

AI, Automation and Traditional Software

## Short description

Learn what actually separates artificial intelligence from automation and ordinary software - and how to tell which one you're looking at.

## Why this matters

"AI" gets used as a label for almost everything right now. Being able to tell what's underneath changes real decisions: what you buy, what you trust, and what you expect a product to do. You don't need a technical background for this - you need a small set of distinctions and the habit of looking for evidence.

## Estimated completion time

About 20 minutes for the core path. Optional deeper reading adds up to 15 more.

## What you'll be able to do

- Describe traditional software, automation, and AI in your own words
- Tell whether an automated system involves AI - or whether you can't tell yet
- Classify everyday systems: your banking app, your email, your navigation app
- Spot when "smart" and "AI-powered" are marketing rather than evidence
- Recognize when a description doesn't give you enough information to judge

## Beginner reassurance

This section assumes nothing. If you've never had a clear explanation of what AI is, that's exactly who this was written for. Guessing wrong along the way is part of how the section works - every answer, right or wrong, comes with the reasoning.
